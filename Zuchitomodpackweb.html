About Zuchito Modpack

Zuchito Modpack is a Fabric pack for Minecraft 26.2 built around smooth, stable gameplay. It bundles the best rendering, memory and chunk-loading optimizers, a polished set of quality-of-life tools, and optional shaders and resource packs so the game looks great without wrecking your frame rate.

What's inside

  • Performance core: Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ModernFix, C2ME, Entity Culling and more.
  • Visuals: Iris with BSL, Complementary Reimagined/Unbound and MakeUp UltraFast shaders, plus sky and grass resource packs.
  • Utilities: Xaero's Minimap and World Map, Zoomify, AppleSkin, Mod Menu, Mouse Tweaks.
  • Customization: FancyMenu for custom menus and title screen.
